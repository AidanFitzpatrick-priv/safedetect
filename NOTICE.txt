SafeDetect is an unofficial personal-use derivative of detector behavior from Meowtils 2.0.1.
Meowtils author: femboytatp
https://github.com/femboytatp/meowtils

AutoBlock, NoSlow, Killaura, and Legit Scaffold checks, the sniper name list, and the
sniper stat rule are adapted from that jar. Gameplay modules from Meowtils are not included.
This project is not Meowtils, and it is not for distribution.
